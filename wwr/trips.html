Our Rafting Trips

Choose from a variety of rafting adventures designed for different levels of experience. Whether you are looking for excitement or a relaxing outdoor experience, we have a trip for you.

Los Dientes del Diablo

Experience an exciting rafting adventure through challenging rapids. This trip is perfect for adventurers looking for excitement and unforgettable memories.

Big Mallard

Enjoy the excitement of the river while experiencing beautiful scenery and thrilling rapids. This adventure is suitable for groups looking for an exciting day on the water.

Half Day Adventure

Short on time? Our Half Day Adventure provides an exciting rafting experience without requiring a full day. It is a great option for families and visitors with limited time.

Rapid Blaster

Get ready for an action-packed river experience. Rapid Blaster is designed for those who want to experience the excitement of whitewater rafting.

Snake River Rush

Take on the river and enjoy an unforgettable adventure. Snake River Rush combines beautiful scenery with exciting whitewater action.
